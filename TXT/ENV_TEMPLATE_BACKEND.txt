======================================== SERVER CONFIG
========================================

PORT=3000 NODE_ENV=development BASE_URL=http://localhost:3000

======================================== GEMINI / GOOGLE AI CONFIG
========================================

Gemini API key from Google AI Studio

GEMINI_API_KEY=PASTE_YOUR_GEMINI_KEY_HERE

Models (easy to change without editing code)

GEMINI_MODEL_FLASH=gemini-3-flash-preview
GEMINI_MODEL_PRO=gemini-2.5-pro
GEMINI_MODEL_TTS=gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts

Optional limits (helps control cost)

AI_MAX_TOKENS=2048 AI_TIMEOUT_MS=30000
