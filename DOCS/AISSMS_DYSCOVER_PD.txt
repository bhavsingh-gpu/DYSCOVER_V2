🔹 DysCover AI — Product Description

DysCover AI is an AI-powered inclusive learning platform that detects early signs of dyslexia and adapts educational content to remove reading barriers for affected students.

Teachers/parents upload handwriting samples or notes → the system screens for dyslexia indicators and transforms materials into dyslexia-friendly formats: simplified text, syllable-highlighted views, paced audio narration, and adaptive quizzes.

Instead of forcing students to struggle with decoding, DysCover adapts the content and experience to the learner — turning frustration into comprehension and confidence.

How it works
• Upload handwriting photo or text/notes/PDF
• Gemini AI analyzes for dyslexia markers (reversals, inconsistent spacing, etc.) with confidence score
• Content processed in cloud: simplified, syllable-split, TTS-ready
• Student accesses via personalized dashboard (audio primary, visual aids, font adjustments)
• Quick quiz after session → AI refines next adaptation (e.g., slower pace, more audio)
• Progress tracked & shared with teachers/parents

Use Cases
Helps:
• Undiagnosed dyslexic students (common in India, often overlooked)
• Slow learners with phonological challenges
• Teachers needing quick screening + remedial tools
• Parents supporting home learning

All from a single, accessible web platform.

Purpose
To bridge the massive diagnosis & support gap for dyslexia — one of the most prevalent yet under-resourced learning disabilities — using affordable AI for true educational equity.

Impact
DysCover acts as an early-intervention layer for millions of Indian students: faster detection, better comprehension, reduced dropout risk, and scalable inclusion in classrooms.
